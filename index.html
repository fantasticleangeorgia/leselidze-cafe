Café Mirabelle was born from a love letter between two storied cultures: the time-honored precision of 1950s French viennoiserie and the boundless hospitality of Old Tbilisi. Every recipe honors slow fermentation, pure Normandy cultured butter, and vibrant regional ingredients from Kakheti and Samegrelo.
